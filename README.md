# What Florida Counties Can Do About a Large Data Center

A free public guide to what Florida counties can do when a large data center is proposed: county standards, mitigation of measured impacts, county land leases, and private community agreements. It is published with the Model Instruments for Data Center Development in Florida Counties, a set of citizen-prepared model instruments for Florida counties.

The model instruments and their methodology are deposited on Zenodo. Releases through 2026.2 were published as the Automation Resilience and Community Benefit Framework.

Citizen-prepared. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by a lawyer. A county should rely on its own attorney before acting on anything here.
