# In-Kind Benefit Screen

A free public guide to what Florida counties can do when a large data center is proposed: county standards, mitigation of measured impacts, county land leases, and private community agreements. It accompanies a set of citizen-prepared model instruments for Florida counties.

The model instruments and their methodology are deposited on Zenodo. Releases through 2026.2 were published as the Automation Resilience and Community Benefit Framework.

Citizen-prepared. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by a lawyer. A county should rely on its own attorney before acting on anything here.
